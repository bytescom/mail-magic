🔒 Why You See a Google Warning

Don't worry - your data is safe!

⚠️ You might see this warning:
"Google hasn't verified this app"

Why Does This Happen?

Google shows this warning for ALL apps that request access to Gmail until they complete a verification process. This process typically takes 4-6 weeks.

✅ Is MailMagic Safe?
Yes! We only use your Gmail access to send emails on your behalf. We NEVER store your emails, passwords, or share your data with anyone. You can revoke access anytime from your Google Account settings.

What We Access & Why:

  • gmail.send - To send job application emails from your account
  • gmail.readonly - To check if emails were sent successfully

How to Proceed Safely:

Step 1: When you see the warning, click "Advanced" at the bottom left
Step 2: Click "Go to mail-magic (unsafe)"
(Yes, the wording is scary, but it just means "not verified yet")
Step 3: Review the permissions and click "Allow"

🔐 Your Security Matters:

  • ✅ We use Google's official OAuth 2.0 (most secure method)
  • ✅ We never see your Gmail password
  • ✅ You can revoke access anytime from your Google Account
  • ✅ We're currently in verification process with Google

Alternative: Wait for Verification

If you prefer to wait, we're actively working with Google to get verified. This process takes 4-6 weeks. Once approved, you won't see this warning anymore!
