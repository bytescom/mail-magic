Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 16, 2026

1. Introduction

MailMagic ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our job application automation service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide:

  • Google Account information (email, name, profile picture)
  • Email templates you create
  • HR contact information you upload
  • Resume/CV files

2.2 Gmail Access:

We request access to your Gmail account to send emails on your behalf. We use the following scopes:

  • gmail.send - To send job application emails from your account
  • gmail.readonly - To track email delivery and responses

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Send job application emails on your behalf
  • Track email delivery status
  • Display analytics about your job applications
  • Improve our service

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored securely in MongoDB databases with encryption
  • Gmail access tokens are encrypted and stored securely
  • We use HTTPS for all data transmission
  • We never share your Gmail access tokens with third parties

5. Gmail API Usage

MailMagic's use of information received from Gmail APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data Retention

  • We retain your data as long as your account is active
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time
  • Gmail access tokens are automatically revoked upon account deletion

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google OAuth 2.0 - For authentication
  • Gmail API - For sending emails
  • MongoDB Atlas - For database storage

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all data
  • Revoke Gmail access at any time
  • Export your data

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 18 years of age.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
